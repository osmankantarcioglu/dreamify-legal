Common questions

How many stories can we create?

One new story per day, per account. The allowance resets at midnight in your device's own time zone. The countdown on the home screen shows exactly when. Stories you already have can be replayed as often as you like; replays never count against the allowance.

What happens if we don't create a story one day?

Nothing is lost and nothing is banked. The last story stays as tonight's story, and the button to create a new one simply stays available.

How do I manage or cancel the subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us. Manage or cancel them in the App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions). Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period continues to its end.

How do I export or delete our data?

Open the Parents tab in the app and use “Delete account and data”. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Is the content safe for young children?

Every story is generated against a fixed set of safety rules and is checked before it reaches you: no violence, no scary imagery, no commercial content, and nothing from your “avoid” list. If something still feels wrong, email us with the story title. We take it seriously.

The narration isn't playing

Narration is prepared the first time a story is opened and takes about a minute. If it stays stuck, close and reopen the story; if it still fails, email us.
